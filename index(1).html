Founder journey

Mrs. Parvati's humble kitchen journey

Mrs. Parvati Swaminathan began her life of service as a school teacher - patient, disciplined and deeply connected with families. A North Indian woman married into a traditional South Indian household, she slowly absorbed the beauty of South Indian cooking through her late mother-in-law's kitchen.

Over the years, she gave those authentic recipes her own North Indian warmth and practical everyday touch. That blend of tradition, care and fusion became Saraswati's Kitchen - a brand created to bring clean, homestyle and delicious food products to modern homes.
